Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

This policy explains what Illume & Co collects when you use Illume Studio, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email or username, and your password stored only as a salted, one-way hash (we can't read it).
  • Your work: clients, projects, prompts, reference images, scripts, notes, storyboards and the images, videos and audio you generate.
  • Credits: your credit balance and history. Card payments are handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.
  • Review links: the name your client types and the comments or approvals they leave.
  • Technical data: basic logs (time, page, errors, device type) to keep the studio working and secure, and anonymous page analytics.

How we use it

Only to run the studio for you: to sign you in, generate and save your work, share it with your team and the clients you choose, process credits, send account emails (such as password resets) and fix problems. We don't use your work to train AI models ourselves, and we don't sell or rent your data.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel — hosting, file and data storage.
  • Atlas Cloud and the AI model makers behind it — your prompts and references are sent to them to generate results.
  • Stripe — payments.
  • Resend — account and notification emails, when enabled.
  • Google Drive — only if you choose to connect it; takes are then copied into your own Drive.

These providers process data under their own privacy terms and may be located outside your country.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. The studio also remembers a few display choices (like light or dark mode) in your browser. No advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Your account and work are kept while your account is open. Deleted takes stay in “Recently deleted” for 14 days, then they're removed. When you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account) your account, work, files, review links and credits are removed; if you were part of a team, the takes you made there stay with the team.

Your choices and rights

You can see, download and delete your work in the studio at any time, and delete your account yourself. Depending on where you live you may have further rights — for example to request a copy of your data or ask us to correct or erase it. Email us and we'll help.

Children

The studio isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.
