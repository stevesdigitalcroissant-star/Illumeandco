Credits & refunds

Last updated October 5, 2026

In short: credits are non-refundable once bought, but you never pay for a generation that fails — those credits come back automatically.

How credits work

  • Credits are bought in packs through Stripe and added to your account right after payment.
  • Every generation shows its cost in credits before you press Generate. Credits are taken when the generation starts.
  • Credits don't expire while your account is open. In a team, generations use the team's shared credits, topped up by the team lead.

Automatic refunds

If a generation fails, is refused by the AI model, or finishes without a result, its credits are returned to your balance automatically. You'll see it as a “refund” line in Settings → Credits.

What isn't refunded

  • Credit packs, once bought — including unused credits.
  • Generations that completed but didn't come out the way you hoped. AI results vary from take to take; the cost shown before each generation is the cost of trying.
  • Credits left in an account that's deleted.

Something went wrong?

If you were charged twice, a payment didn't add credits, or a generation clearly failed without a refund, email illumeandco.online@gmail.com with your account email and roughly when it happened. We'll look into it and put it right. Nothing here limits any refund rights you have under the law where you live.
