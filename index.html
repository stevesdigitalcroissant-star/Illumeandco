Who it’s for — and who it isn’t.

The Studio Plan is for

  • Businesses that already have customers and want to look as good as they are.
  • Owners who want their products and services to look the part, without a shoot or a studio.
  • Restaurants, cafés, beauty and personal care, home-based food brands, trades and service businesses.

It isn’t for

  • Anyone looking for overnight viral results.
  • Anyone who wants to hand over an ad budget and disappear.
  • Businesses that don’t want to be involved in their own marketing.

A few things people ask

What’s included in the Studio Plan?

Fresh, original Instagram pictures, carousels and reels created for the product or service you deliver, posted for you on a steady rhythm, with captions and a monthly content plan built around your goals. Plus promotional videos, ready-to-run ads, your website built or fixed from month two, and ongoing direction — we plan each month together. The scope flexes to what your business needs that month.

Do you just re-edit the content I already have?

No. We create fresh, original content built around your products and services. A simple phone photo is all we need to start — the finished creative is new work, made by us. You never run out of content for us to work with, because we’re not working from your old posts.

What happens in month one?

Your Launch Month. In week one we review your brand, your products and how you currently show up, and build your content plan. Throughout the month we create and post your content, so your pages come alive while we work. At the end of the month we deliver your promotional video, made once we know your brand.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The Studio Plan is month to month — we just ask for 7 days’ notice before your next billing date. Everything we made for you is yours to keep, always.

Is there a refund?

No. The plan is month to month, so there’s nothing to lock you in. If after your Launch Month you don’t see the value, simply don’t continue — and keep everything we made.

Do you run my ads?

We create ready-to-run ads for Instagram, Facebook, Google and WhatsApp. You keep control of your ad account and budget — we make what goes in it. Ad spend is never included in the plan.

Can you build my website?

Yes — built or fixed. We take that on from your second month, once we know your brand.

Is it really $950 — will it go up?

$950 a month, in USD, is our founding rate. It will rise as we grow. You’re invoiced at the start of each month, and work begins once the month is paid.

Do you use AI to make the work?

Yes — AI is part of our toolkit, the same way editing software and colour grading are. Your products, your brand and your direction shape everything, and every piece is art-directed and reviewed by us before it reaches you. AI assists the craft; it doesn’t replace it.

Who am I working with?

The two of us. We lead every project personally — no account managers, no middle layer.

What do I need to send?

A few phone photos or videos of your product, space or service — we create the finished content from there; partner access to your Instagram and Facebook pages through Meta Business Suite (never your password); and about 30 minutes for a kickoff conversation.

Ready to have your marketing handled?

Tell us about your business — we’ll reply personally and plan your Launch Month together.
