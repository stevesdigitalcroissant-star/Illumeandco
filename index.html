Behind the studio

Illume was started after seeing how many genuinely good small businesses were represented online by nothing more than a quick phone photo, simply because a proper shoot cost more than a week's sales.

The studio exists to close that gap — taking what a business already has and giving it the polish it deserves, without agency overhead or agency pricing.

A few things people ask

What exactly is included in the $250?

6 creative assets built from your existing content and tailored to your marketing needs, captions written for each one, and 1 professional promotional video — all delivered within 7 days. This is open to our first three founding clients.

Do you use AI to make the work?

Yes — AI is part of our toolkit, the same way editing software and colour grading are. Your content, your brand and your direction shape everything, and every piece is art-directed and reviewed by us before it reaches you.

What do I need to send?

Photos and video of your product, space or team — whatever you already have. We'll tell you what's most useful after our first conversation.

What happens after the 7 days?

If it's working for you, we'll talk about an ongoing plan built around your business — no fixed package, no pressure to continue.
